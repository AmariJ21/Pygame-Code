How to read the moves

  1. Each letter is a side: U top, D bottom, R right, L left, F front, B back.
  2. A letter alone means turn that side clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it. ' means counter-clockwise. 2 means turn it twice.
  3. Never turn the whole cube while you follow along. The plain words under the big letter tell you which way to turn.
  4. Keys: → next, ← back, Space play or pause.
