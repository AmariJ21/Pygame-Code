How to read the moves

  1. Each letter is a side: U top, D bottom, R right, L left, F front, B back.
  2. A letter alone means turn that side clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it. ' means counter-clockwise. 2 means turn it twice.
  3. Never turn the whole cube while you follow along. The plain words under the big letter tell you which way to turn.
  4. Keys: → next, ← back, Space play or pause.

Bill direction map and solved cube

Money cube

Center pictures spin freely, so they can't tell you which way a face points. Use this instead:

  1. Build one bill by eye. Hold it with the Franklin half facing you, upright, and its seal half on the right.
  2. Look only at the kind of the other centers (Franklin or seal), not which way they point. Pick the case below that matches where the other two Franklin centers are.
  3. Every face now has a fixed direction. "Top of the bill" is where "100 FEDERAL RESERVE NOTE" or "THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA" goes. The fold edge is where Franklin's face goes, or the "UN / O", eye and "ONE HU" column on a seal face.

1 · Photograph each face

For picture cubes

Take a straight-on photo of each face. The page cuts it into 9 squares so you can see your real $100 pieces below. Photos stay on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Line up the face

Drag the photo and use the zoom slider until the 9 squares sit inside the grid lines.

2 · Match each square to its face

Click or drag

Every face's picture is set by its middle square, which never moves. Pick a center below, then click every square whose piece of $100 bill belongs on that face. A square's colored tag shows what you picked. No photos? The colors work on their own too.

3 · Fix twisted centers

After the colors are solved
